A multilingual MD A6 notebook: layout ideas

Русская версия

Page mockups at true A6 proportions (105 × 148 mm), 176 blank pages: English, Polish, Spanish, Basque, Belarusian, a reserve for one more language, and a little Latin. The style switch changes every spread at once, so the styles are easy to compare.

How the 176 pages are split

Order in the notebook: EN → PL → ES → EU → BE → reserve for a new language → LA → shared spreads → open section → lists written from the back. Hover over a segment to see its page range.

Language colours (Okabe–Ito palette)

These colours stay distinguishable for the most common types of colour-vision deficiency. Yellow is left out: a thin yellow line is almost invisible on cream paper.

EN — blue PL — vermilion ES — orange EU — sky blue BE — bluish green +1 — dashed grey until the language is chosen LA — reddish purple ∑ shared — black
